About Cityline Travels

Established in 2021, Cityline Travels has become one of the trusted names in bus rental and transportation services in Mumbai and Thane.

We provide transportation solutions for corporate companies, schools, colleges, industrial staff, weddings, family trips, tourism, and special events.

Customer satisfaction, timely service, vehicle cleanliness, and professional drivers are the foundation of our business. Every vehicle undergoes regular maintenance and safety inspections to provide a hassle-free travel experience.

Whether you need a bus for local travel or an outstation trip, Cityline Travels offers reliable transportation at competitive rates.
